Your first ten minutes, every day

  1. Read one sentence

    How loud your brand is against its rivals, and how that moved since yesterday, on every search term you track.

  2. Clear the reply inbox

    Safety scares, consumer-court threats and products that stopped working come first, each with the exact words that triggered it.

  3. Ask what you need

    "Why do people complain about the service?" Answers cite real comments, show how they were built, and only use numbers from the data.

Fanvaaz is fan plus awaaz, Hindi for voice: what your fans are saying about you. Data from the YouTube Data API. Personal details in comments are removed before anything is stored.
